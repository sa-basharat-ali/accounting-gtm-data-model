A-1 ⊗New vs expansion

The Type field turns expansions into new logos

Expansions land on duplicate accounts or get typed as new business. Fixing the field alone still leaves +30%. Only resolving the firm first gets it right, and the same fix moves customers from 24 to 20 and customers live in two or more practices from 6 to 9.

A-2 ⊗Identity

"Who is the customer" has no automatic answer

Domain matching catches 15 of 22 duplicate accounts with no false merges. Name matching catches all 22 but merges 3 different companies that share a name. The design that works is tiered: auto-merge on domain, send the 10 name-only matches to a person (7 real, 3 not).

Domain: caught15 / 22
Name: caught22 / 22
Name: wrong merges3
A-3 ⊗Attribution

Partner and AE both claim the deal

13 partner-registered deals were also marked AE-sourced. One written rule, a registration dated before the opportunity was created wins, gives every deal exactly one motion and flags all 13 with no false flags.

CRM + partner report summed$5.82M
Actual pipeline + bookings$5.07M
A-4 ⊗Activity

Retries hide from the usual dedup

150 webhook retries reused their event ID; 67 regenerated it. Dedup on event_id misses every regenerated one; dedup on message_id catches both. Then the definition: sends are attempts, not engagement.

"Engaged" firms, any touch214
Engaged: reply or held call95
A-5 ⊗Sync drift

A stalled sync makes healthy customers look dark

For 10 accounts, the Salesforce last-activity sync stopped weeks ago while calls kept happening in Gong. A drift check comparing the field to its sources flags all 10 with no false flags.

A-6 ✓Agents

Agents get context, not the keys

The ontology records each entity's grain, which system owns each field, the metric rules in plain language, and what each agent may do. Reads carry lineage; governed changes go to a person with evidence attached.

sdr_agent     task.create          applied
revops_agent  firm.merge           escalated
sdr_agent     last_engaged.update  blocked
              (owned by warehouse)
The part I got wrong

The first version caught every failure with zero false merges, because every duplicate shared a domain. That was the generator agreeing with the detector, not a result. Adding duplicates under a legacy domain from an acquired firm produced the 15/22 and the false merges above, and the tiered design came out of that.

D-1

First 90 days in the role

DAYS 1–30

Find out what is true

  • Map which system controls each field across Salesforce, Outreach, Gong and Clay.
  • Resolve accounts into firms and buying groups; size the duplicate queue.
  • Agree the expansion rule and the partner-credit rule with RevOps and Finance.
DAYS 31–60

Make the numbers agree

  • Ship the semantic layer for pipeline, new vs expansion ARR, conversion and customer health.
  • Reconcile reporting to source records so teams stop rebuilding numbers in spreadsheets.
  • Add drift and sync monitoring with alerts.
DAYS 61–90

Give agents safe context

  • Publish the GTM ontology behind permission-aware interfaces.
  • Let agents propose merges and fixes; people approve governed changes.
  • Report practice penetration by firm so expansion is visible.
